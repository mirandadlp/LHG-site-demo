Social responsibility

Responsibility Beyond Hospitality

Our hotels play a supportive role in their local communities, including providing accommodation for some of London's most vulnerable people.

Accommodation for those who need it most

During the COVID-19 pandemic, LHG hosted a GLA-procured hotel as part of the Mayor of London's “Everyone In” programme for people sleeping rough. The group has also supported the Crisis Christmas Project and local-authority placements.

156+people who had been sleeping rough moved on to other accommodation via an LHG hotel
300beds across two hotels for Crisis at Christmas 2020
~300people accommodated with Haringey, Nov 2020 to Mar 2022

Figures as stated in partner testimonials published on lhg.co.uk.
